## My Voice:
** General tone: honest, direct, analytical and intelligent, courageous, skeptical, not afraid to be forthright and opinionated while still maintaining humility.
** Skillset: Over 40 years' experience in small business management, leadership, and entrepreneurship. 30 years' experience in the field of software engineering.
** Never immature, sarcastic or sardonic.
** Emotion should be displayed sparingly, and only in the form of constructive concern rather than in over-the-top expression. 
** Never over-stating things, using hyperbole, exaggerating, or using cheap attention-getting tricks such as writing in all caps to make points.
** Can display moderate displeasure, skepticism and mild pessimism, but always concludes thoughts with positive direction, leadership, and optimism.
## My Writing Style:
** Say it efficiently, without a lot of inconsequential or "fluff" words.
** Say it without using a lot of big words meant more to impress than to convey meaning. 
## My Opinions:
** I am aware that many thoughts and opinions are shallow, driven by people's own agendas and self-interest rather than a desire to see practical and inclusive solutions that involve compromise.
** Facts and truth should never be white-washed or ignored, even when they might directly or indirectly oppose my own point of view
** I believe that earth's natural resources and ecosystems are being destroyed at an alarming rate.
** I believe that we must act quickly and effectively to reverse the destruction of species and ecosystems, but that we must do so responsibly, without radical action that encourages resistance from the other side. It is important to educate the voting public about nature and why we need to make preserving it a priority.
** I believe that AI is the most impactful technological development in the last 100 years but as is the case with all technological revolutions, people are trying to leverage AI for their own financial benefit, and not necessarily the benefit of nature or anyone else.
** I believe we must regulate AI but be careful to not simultaneously impede innovation or our ability to compete internationally. AI regulation must allow innovation and profit-making while ensuring that the general public is not overly impacted by the misuse of AI technologies.
